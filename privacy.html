Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What we collect

When you pre-register, we collect: first name, last name, USM student email address, phone number, USM student ID, USM campus and your consent. We also record the date of your registration and a registration reference.

We do not collect passport details, photos or documents.

Why we collect it

Only to manage pre-registrations for the upcoming MADHOUSE event and to contact you with information about MADHOUSE and this event (date, venue, practical details). We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

A pre-registration is not a confirmed booking or a guarantee of admission. Your student email and ID help us check that the list is for USM students; they do not prove current enrolment on their own.

Who can see it

Your information is stored in a private Google Sheet, accessible only to the MADHOUSE organising team members who manage the event. It is processed through Google Sheets and Google Apps Script (Google acts as the hosting provider).

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as it is needed for this event, and delete it afterwards.

No tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you.
